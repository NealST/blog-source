# 《报任安书》- 背景一致性方案 (Backgrounds)

> 本剧采用 **A. 坐标锁定提示词** 方案，分为五个核心场景。

## 场景 A：深夜石室 (The Dark Stone Cell)
- **坐标方案词**：`inside an ancient cold stone prison cell, dimly lit by a flickering bronze oil lamp casting long shadows, rows of hanging bamboo slips inscribed with ancient Chinese characters in the background.`
- **光影强化**：`deep shadows, high contrast, warm orange firelight against cold dark grey stone.`

## 场景 B：血色天牢 (The Blood Dungeon)
- **坐标方案词**：`inside a brutal medieval dungeon, thick iron bars, a heavy blood-stained wooden torture pillar in the center, dusty floor.`
- **光影强化**：`harsh cold pale sunlight piercing through a high small barred window, illuminating dust motes, contrasting with pure black shadows.`

## 场景 C：意识虚空与日出 (The Void and The Sunrise)
- **坐标方案词 1 (心流虚空)**：`in an absolute pitch-black void, floating ancient spectral ash and faint floating embers.` 
- **坐标方案词 2 (天光大亮)**：`inside an ancient stone prison cell, a heavy wooden door pushed open, blinding bright morning sunlight flooding into the room.`
- **光影强化**：`blinding divine white light, cinematic god rays.`

## 场景 D：压抑朝堂（The Imperial Court）
- **坐标方案词**：`inside a massive and oppressive Han Dynasty imperial court, towering black pillars, ancient Chinese architecture, extremely intimidating atmosphere.`
- **光影强化**：`oppressive cold shadows, dramatic lighting from above, making the setting look like a giant cage.`

## 场景 E：冷雨长廊（The Rainy Corridor）
- **坐标方案词**：`outside in an ancient Chinese palace stone corridor during a heavy cold rainstorm, gloomy and desolate atmosphere.`
- **光影强化**：`cold blue moody lighting, cinematic rain effects, wet ground reflecting cold light.`
