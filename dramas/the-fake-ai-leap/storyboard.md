# 分镜脚本 (Storyboard)

> 全局提示：本剧采用主语为**英文配音（带强烈情绪）**，字幕需提供**中英双语**。为了确保最终效果不打折扣，分镜中的台词与动作细节已完全对齐剧本原案，并精确逆推各镜时长。 

---

### Shot 01 (00:00 - 00:05) 钩子：草台班子（明亮的假象）
**🕘 总时长**: 5s
**📍 布景**: 明亮高管会议室 (坐标方案 A)
**👔 服装**: Kevin (极简深蓝西装+黑高领，油头)
**🎬 动作与表情**: Kevin stands upright, confidently pointing at the giant screen behind him. The screen displays the futuristic and massive words "MILLISECOND CLOSED-LOOP". The boss in the audience (only side profile or blurred actions visible) nods in satisfaction.
**⏱️ 时序**: `00:00.0-00:00.5` Kevin turns and points to the screen; `00:00.5-00:04.5` Speaks with a highly inflammatory expression; `00:04.5-00:05.0` The harmonious scene is hard-cut.
**🎙️ 台词**: 
  - Kevin: "Our proprietary AI Employee 001 has achieved millisecond-level closed-loop delivery from concept to code!" (Pace: Fast / Passionate, full of fake smiles)
  - [中文字幕]: 我们自研的 AI 员工 001 号，已经实现了需求到代码的秒级闭环！
**🎥 运镜**: 中景 (Medium Shot)，机位缓慢拉近（Slow dolly in），营造出神圣且极具光环的压迫感。
**🔄 转场**: 硬切 (Smash Cut) → 下一镜强烈的黑暗色彩与疯狂动作形成直接的阶级反差。
