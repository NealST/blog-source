# 背景一致性方案 (Background & Settings)

本剧采用 **方案 A：坐标锁定提示词**，通过在各镜头的 prompt 中复述具体的环境描述来保持场景一致。

## 场景清单与背景提示词

### 📍 坐标方案 A：明亮高管会议室 (Bright Conference Room)
- **适用镜头**：Kevin 的吹嘘与汇报、最终高光时刻。
- **环境描述 (Setting)**: `A modern, overly bright corporate conference room with clinical white walls, a massive holographic presentation screen in the background, sleek glass table, oppressive clean capitalist aesthetic.`

### 📍 坐标方案 B：昏暗底层工位 (Dark Cubicle)
- **适用镜头**：林工的日常位、熬夜修 Bug、最后瘫倒的场景。
- **环境描述 (Setting)**: `A claustrophobic, dark cubicle workspace illuminated only by the harsh green and blue glow of multiple computer monitors screens, messy desk covered with empty coffee cups, wires, and error message red lights, deep shadows.`

### 📍 坐标方案 C：冰冷晋升答辩室 (Cold Promotion Room)
- **适用镜头**：林工汇报被拒。
- **环境描述 (Setting)**: `A cold, intimidating corporate assessment room, dim overhead spotlight casting long shadows, a single projector screen showing data, creating a high-pressure interrogation atmosphere.`
