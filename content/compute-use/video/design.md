# Design

## Mood
Dark editorial technical sketchbook. Precise engineering diagrams meet the slightly absurd hand-drawn Xiaohei illustrations from the article.

## Colors
- Background: `#1C1C1E`
- Foreground: `#F2EDE3`
- Accent: `#D4A76A`
- Panel: `#2C2A28`
- Muted: `#8D8983`
- Signal Red: `#D7655B`
- Signal Blue: `#78A6B8`
- Paper: `#F4F0E7`

## Typography
- Display / Numbers: DM Sans, 800
- Body Chinese: PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei, sans-serif
- Code / Labels: JetBrains Mono, 500

## Layout
- Format: vertical 1080x1920
- Platform safety zone: 180px top, 120px bottom
- Watermark: top 224px, left 58px
- Main content: 320px top, 64px sides, 100px bottom

## Constraints
- Chinese first; English only for model and protocol names.
- Use gold for one focal path or phrase per scene.
- Use paper illustrations as large editorial anchors, never small thumbnails.
- No pure white, neon glow, gradient text, nested cards, or dense paragraphs.
- Duration: 88 seconds, 8 scenes, capped below the 90-second platform limit.
