# Agent Frameworks 2026 — Video Design

## Format

- Canvas: 1080 × 1920, vertical social video
- Duration: 90 seconds
- Mood: technical editorial, calm authority, precise rather than futuristic
- Density: cinematic infographic; large type, structural rules, layered diagrams

## Palette

- Background: `#1C1C1E`
- Foreground: `#F2EDE3`
- Accent: `#C4956A`
- Accent light: `#D8B28C`
- Secondary text: `#B8B0A4`
- Panel: `#272528`
- Rule: `#4A4540`

## Typography

- Display: `"ZCOOL XiaoWei", serif`, weight 400
- Body: `"Noto Sans SC", sans-serif`, weight 300–700
- Technical labels: `"IBM Plex Mono", monospace`, weight 500–700
- Headlines: 76–112px
- Body: 32–42px
- Labels: 22–26px

## Shape and Depth

- Corners: 8–16px, never pill-shaped
- Borders: 2–3px
- Depth: flat panels with localized warm glow; no generic drop-shadow cards
- Texture: subtle grid, grain, oversized ghost terms, registration marks

## Motion

- Editorial push and blur-through transitions
- One cinematic zoom accent before the decision scene
- Entrances vary between line draws, directional slides, scale stamps, and type reveals
- Ambient movement is slow and deterministic: grid drift, glow breath, diagram pulse

## Brand Elements

- Persistent watermark: `公众号：墨筝`
- Warm gold rules and registration marks
- Final CTA: `完整对比与选型细节，请关注公众号「墨筝」`
- BGM volume: `0.3`

## Avoid

- Cyan or purple neon
- Gradient text
- Generic equal-sized card grids
- Centered web-page layouts
- Tiny UI text
- Excessive glow
- Emojis
