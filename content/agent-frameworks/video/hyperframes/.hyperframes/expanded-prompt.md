# AI Agent Frameworks in 2026 — Production Breakdown

## Style

Use the exact design tokens from `design.md`: background `#1C1C1E`, foreground `#F2EDE3`, accent `#C4956A`, accent light `#D8B28C`, secondary text `#B8B0A4`, panel `#272528`, and rule `#4A4540`. Display type uses Songti SC; body uses PingFang SC; technical labels use SF Mono.

The video is a 1080×1920, 90-second technical editorial teaser. It should feel like a confident magazine explainer translated into motion, not a software dashboard.

## Rhythm

`hook-PUNCH-build-hold-build-PEAK-breathe-CTA`

Eight scenes:

1. Hook — 8s
2. Production gap — 11s
3. Three-layer map — 11s
4. Framework choices — 12s
5. Vendor runtimes — 11s
6. MCP vs A2A — 11s
7. Decision routes — 14s
8. Lock-in and CTA — 12s

## Global Rules

- Keep all essential content inside the vertical safe area.
- Use at least three depth layers per scene: textured background, primary content, foreground marks.
- Every scene receives distinct entrance choreography with at least three easing signatures.
- Use editorial push transitions as the primary handoff.
- Use blur-through for conceptual continuations.
- Use one cinematic zoom accent into the decision scene.
- Do not animate scene elements out before transitions. Only the final scene may fade.
- Maintain a persistent `公众号：墨筝` watermark at the top safe area.
- Use `content/_shared/bgm.mp3` at volume `0.3`.

## Scene 1 — The Question

### Concept

The viewer enters a wall of framework names. LangGraph, CrewAI, OpenAI Agents SDK, Google ADK, MCP, and A2A arrive from different directions until the central question stamps over them: “到底该选哪个？” The feeling is recognizable confusion, immediately organized by editorial authority.

### Mood

Opening titles from a serious technology documentary; restrained but forceful.

### Depth

- BG: drifting grid, oversized ghost word `AGENT`, localized gold glow
- MG: six framework labels at different scales
- FG: title rule, issue number `2026`, registration ticks

### Choreography

- `2026` types on in SF Mono.
- Framework names SLIDE and DROP into the field with varied directions.
- A gold rule DRAWS across the frame.
- “到底该选哪个？” STAMPS into place last.

### Transition

Editorial upward push, 0.45s, `power3.inOut`.

## Scene 8 — The Real Lock-In

### Concept

The final scene slows down. A lightweight framework box disconnects easily, but state, sandbox, and session history remain chained to the runtime. The video ends on the question of ownership at 3 a.m., followed by the WeChat CTA.

### Mood

Quiet warning, then confident resolution.

### Depth

- BG: slow warm glow, deep grid, ghost clock `03:00`
- MG: replaceable framework block and anchored runtime core
- FG: chain lines, ownership question, CTA, watermark

### Choreography

- Framework block FLOATS away.
- Runtime core SETTLES with visual weight.
- Three chains DRAW toward `状态 / 沙盒 / 会话`.
- `03:00` COUNTS into view.
- The ownership question TYPES on slowly.
- CTA and gold rule FADE in last.
- Final scene gently fades toward black.
